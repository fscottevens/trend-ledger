How This Works

Every S&P 500 and S&P 400 stock is re-scanned automatically during market hours against several well-known, published trading frameworks — not a single black-box signal, but a blend of technical trend criteria, growth-stock fundamentals, and momentum triggers drawn from public sources.

The results split into two philosophically different views. Momentum Leaders ranks names using live intraday price and volume action — the breakout, volume-surge, and relative-strength triggers day traders watch for right now. Trend Quality & Value re-ranks the same qualifying pool using fundamentals and traditional valuation metrics instead, favoring names with earnings growth, reasonable debt, and institutional support alongside a healthy technical trend.

Neither list is a signal to buy or sell. Select any stock to see exactly why it ranked where it did — every moving average, every return period, every criterion met or missed — and use that as a starting point for your own research, not the end of it.

Methodology & Disclosure. This screen ranks securities automatically against public-domain approximations of published trading frameworks.

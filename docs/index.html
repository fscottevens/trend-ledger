How This Works

Roughly 900 stocks — every S&P 500 and S&P 400 constituent — are re-scanned automatically each hour during market hours and ranked against several published trading frameworks: Minervini's Trend Template, O'Neil's CANSLIM, and momentum criteria associated with Dan Zanger and Qullamaggie. Nothing is hand-picked. If a name appears here, it is because it scored here.

The results split into two deliberately different views. Momentum Leaders ranks on live intraday price and volume — the breakout and relative-strength signals short-term traders watch. Trend Quality & Value re-ranks the same pool on fundamentals and traditional valuation instead. Why both exist is covered in Momentum vs. Value.

Neither list is a signal to buy or sell anything. Select any stock to see what the company does and precisely why it ranked where it did — every moving average, return period, and criterion met or missed. You can also look up any of the ~900 scanned stocks, and the History page shows every past list and what happened next. If this is your first visit, start with How to Read the Screen, which covers what this data does not tell you.

Educational tool — not investment advice. These rankings are the mechanical output of automated filters applied to delayed public market data. They are not recommendations, and they do not account for your financial situation or risk tolerance. Market data is delayed roughly 15–20 minutes and may contain errors.

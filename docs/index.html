How This Works

Every S&P 500 and S&P 400 stock is re-scanned automatically during market hours and ranked against several published trading frameworks — Minervini's Trend Template, O'Neil's CANSLIM, and momentum criteria associated with Dan Zanger and Qullamaggie. Nothing is hand-picked: if a name appears here, it is because it scored here.

The results split into two philosophically different views. Momentum Leaders ranks using live intraday price and volume action — the breakout and relative-strength signals short-term traders watch. Trend Quality & Value re-ranks the same pool using fundamentals and traditional valuation instead. The difference between them is explained in Momentum vs. Value.

Neither list is a signal to buy or sell anything. Select any stock to see exactly why it ranked where it did — every moving average, return period, and criterion met or missed. New here? Start with How to Read the Screen, which covers what this data does not tell you.

Educational tool — not investment advice. These rankings are the mechanical output of automated filters applied to delayed public market data. They are not recommendations, and they do not account for your financial situation or risk tolerance. Market data is delayed roughly 15–20 minutes and may contain errors. Trend Template

Each segment is one of Minervini's 8 criteria: price above rising 150/200-day averages, 50-day above both, price 30%+ off its 52-week low and within 25% of its high, and RS Rating ≥ 70. Full explanation →

CANSLIM

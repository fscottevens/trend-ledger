Guides to the frameworks behind the screen — what they measure, why, and where they fail. · Last updated September 5, 2026

The screen on this site ranks stocks against several published trading frameworks. These guides explain what those frameworks actually say, why their rules exist, and where they break down — so the rankings are something you can evaluate for yourself rather than take on faith.

Everything here is educational. None of it is advice, and none of it should be treated as a reason to buy or sell anything.

Suggested reading order

If you are new to this style of screening, the guides build on each other in this order:

  1. How to Read the Screen — orients you to what you are looking at and, more importantly, what it does not tell you.
  2. The Trend Template — the primary ranking factor for both lists, so understanding it explains most of what drives the order.
  3. CANSLIM — where the fundamental factors come from and why they sit alongside technical ones.
  4. Momentum vs. Value — the philosophical difference between the two lists.
  5. Glossary — reference material to return to as needed.
A note on what these guides are. They are explanations of publicly published trading frameworks, written to help you evaluate an automated screen critically. They are not investment advice, not a course, and not a claim that any of these approaches will make money. Every framework described here has produced significant losses in some market conditions, and none is endorsed by or affiliated with its originator.
